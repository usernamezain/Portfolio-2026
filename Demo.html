About

I'm Zain, a creative developer. I turn design ideas into fast, accessible websites and add motion where it helps the reader, not where it only decorates.

I work with React, Next.js and TypeScript on the frontend, GSAP and Lenis for scroll and motion, and Three.js when a project needs 3D. I also write small npm packages and CLI tools. I speak Punjabi, English and Urdu.

I'm open to freelance projects and collaborations. Get in touch.
